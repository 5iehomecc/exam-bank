## Unit5+Unit6+Unit7 A School Trip

学校旅行是你享受欢乐时光，开阔视野，增进友谊的集体活动。请回忆一次你印象最深的学校旅行，根据以下问题描写记录难忘的行程。（描述活动时注意使用一般过去时！）选用词汇（visit, pick, learn, buy, be interested in）

1. Where did you go on the school trip?
2. What did you do there?
3. Did you feel happy? How did you feel?

### 基础版：

Last month we had a wonderful school trip by bus. It was unforgettable.

In Da Xing District there is a beautiful farm. We went there to learn some skills about farm work and tried cooking simple local food.

In the morning, with the help of the friendly farmers, we fed some chicken and picked some strawberries. We had a good time.

Then we picked up some vegetables and cooked lunch in groups. I thought the food we cooked was delicious, although we were not good at cooking.

After lunch, we helped the farmers do some farm work. It was my first time to use farm tools. I felt happy when the farmers showed their thanks.

All in all, it was a nice trip. It is so meaningful that I can't forget it.

### 挑战版：

### A school trip to a farm in Huairou

Last month we had a meaningful school trip to a farm in Huairou. We got there fast by bus. It was the first time to walk out of the school with our classmates and teachers. We sang songs happily and saw some beautiful sceneries along the way.

As soon as we arrived at the farm, I was attracted by the beautiful lake nearby. Firstly, we picked quite a lot of tomatoes and strawberries happily. Then, we cooked dishes in groups. I thought the food was really delicious. After lunch, the friendly farmers taught us some skills about farming. We learnt how to plough(种地), studied the seeds, made steamed bread and tofu, fed the chickens and milked cows. Using farm tools made me feel enjoyable and meaningful because I made great efforts to taste the joy of labor.

In the afternoon, we went to a big park to do orienteering in different groups. Our group lost our way when arriving at the fourth controlled point. Although we used the phone to navigate, we

couldn't find the place. Then, we worked together to think carefully and searched for the directions with the clues and maps. Finally we made it! What impressed me most was jumping ropes while holding the water cups. We tried 3 times and finished 15 continuous jumps without dropping the water! In the end, we ran so fast in time and got the prizel! It was meaningful to realize the importance of teamwork! Tired but happy, we went back home.

All in all, it was a meaningful trip. As the old saying goes that, read thousands of books, travel thousands of miles! It is meaningful because it not only broadened my horizon but also improved our friendship. It was like a precious gift in my mind and I will cherish it forever. Looking forward to the next trip!

## Unit8 Telling stories

假设 China Daily 网站中的 Culture 板块中有一项 “Tell a Chinese traditional story” 征文活动。假如你是李华，请你以 “My favorite Chinese traditional story” 为题写一篇英语文章进行投稿。

提示：1. 描述故事内容。2. 故事带给你的影响和启发。

要求：1. 不得在作文中出现学校的真实名称、老师和同学的真实姓名 2. 语句连贯，字数 100 词左右。

#### 基础版：

#### My favorite Chinese traditional story

There are many traditional stories in China. Among all of the stories, I like YuGong moves the mountains best.

Once upon a time, there was an old man called YuGong. There were two high mountains near his home. They were so high that it took him a long time to walk to the other side. So he decided to move the mountains. YuGong and his family began to move the mountains. They kept moving them day after day, year after year. Finally, a god was so moved that he took the mountains away.

The story reminds us that nothing is impossible as long as we work hard. We should not give up.

#### Sample2:

Traditional stories are an important part of Chinese culture.

We can learn a lot from these stories. Let me tell you a famous one called Yu Gong Moves a Mountain.

Once upon a time, there was a very old man called Yu Gong. There were two mountains near his house. They were so big that it took a long time to walk to the side. To solve this problem, Yu Gong wanted to move the mountains. So he and his family worked day after day, year after year. Finally, a god was so moved by their spirit that he sent two gods to take the mountains away.

This story tells us that we should never give up when we meet difficulties.
