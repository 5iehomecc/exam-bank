
## 第一部分 听力理解（共30分）

### 一、听后选择。（每小题1.5分，共15分）

请听 5 段对话或独白，每段对话或独白后有两道小题，请从每题所给的 A、B、C 三个选项中选择最佳选项。每段对话或独白你将听两遍。

请听一段对话，完成第1至第2小题。

1. Who did the boy visit last weekend?

A. His uncle.
B. His teachers.
C. His grandparents.

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】C
> 
> 【考点】听力细节信息获取——人物关系
> 
> 【解析】
> 题干关键词是 "Who ... visit last weekend"，听音时要抓住 visit 后的宾语。
> - 选项A：His uncle 与听力原文不符；
> - 选项B：His teachers 与听力原文不符；
> - 选项C：His grandparents 与原文"周末看望祖父母"的信息一致。
> 
> 【易错点】uncle、teachers、grandparents 三个词都可指人，听到亲属称谓时容易混淆，要听清具体身份。

2. What did the boy do on Sunday?

A. He went fishing.
B. He picked apples.
C. He watered vegetables.

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】B
> 
> 【考点】听力细节信息获取——动作行为
> 
> 【解析】
> 题干问 "What did the boy do on Sunday?"，重点听星期天对应的活动。
> - 选项A：He went fishing 与原文不符；
> - 选项B：He picked apples 与原文一致；
> - 选项C：He watered vegetables 与原文不符。
> 
> 【易错点】fishing、picking apples、watering vegetables 同属农场类活动，容易因听到同类词而误选，要听清动作对象。

请听一段对话，完成第3至第4小题。

3. When will the woman probably get home today?

A. Around 8:30 p.m.
B. Around 9:30 p.m.
C. Around 10:30 p.m.

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】B
> 
> 【考点】听力细节信息获取——时间推算
> 
> 【解析】
> 三个选项时间相差一小时，听音时记录"下班/出发时间 + 路上用时"，再推算到家时间。
> - 选项A：Around 8:30 p.m. 推算偏早，与原文不符；
> - 选项B：Around 9:30 p.m. 与原文推算结果一致；
> - 选项C：Around 10:30 p.m. 推算偏晚，与原文不符。
> 
> 【易错点】听力材料中常先给出一个时间再补充延迟信息，只记住第一个时间就会错选，要听到最后。

4. What will the boy do?

A. Pack up things.
B. Sweep the floor.
C. Clean the kitchen.

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】B
> 
> 【考点】听力细节信息获取——家务分工
> 
> 【解析】
> 题干问男孩将要做什么，注意对话中"分配任务"的句型（如 You do... / I'll...）。
> - 选项A：Pack up things 与原文不符；
> - 选项B：Sweep the floor 与原文一致；
> - 选项C：Clean the kitchen 是对话中提到的另一项家务，不是男孩的任务。
> 
> 【易错点】对话中同时出现多项家务时，要分清"谁做什么"，抓主语而不是只抓动作词。

请听一段对话，完成第5至第6小题。

5. What animals does the boy choose for his homework?

A. Wolves.
B. Cheetahs.
C. Monkeys.

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】B
> 
> 【考点】听力细节信息获取——动物名称
> 
> 【解析】
> 题干问男孩为作业选择了哪种动物，听音时锁定动物名词及其复现。
> - 选项A：Wolves 与原文不符；
> - 选项B：Cheetahs 与原文一致；
> - 选项C：Monkeys 与原文不符。
> 
> 【易错点】wolves 与 cheetahs 都以复数形式出现，且常与 monkeys 并列出现在同一段材料中，要听清"男孩选定"的那一个。

6. What will the girl do first?

A. Take a class.
B. Read some books.
C. Look up information online.

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】A
> 
> 【考点】听力细节信息获取——动作顺序
> 
> 【解析】
> 题干中的 first 是解题关键，必须抓住表示先后顺序的 first / then / after that。
> - 选项A：Take a class 是女孩最先做的事，与原文一致；
> - 选项B：Read some books 是之后才做的事；
> - 选项C：Look up information online 是最后才做的事。
> 
> 【易错点】三个选项都是材料中真实出现的动作，只是先后顺序不同，若不注意 first 就会误选。

请听一段独白，完成第7至第8小题。

7. What do we learn from the talk?

A. The library has become larger.
B. There were exercise machines at the old gym.
C. The dining hall served rice noodles last year.

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】A
> 
> 【考点】听力细节信息获取——事实判断
> 
> 【解析】
> 题干问"从讲话中了解到什么"，属于事实细节题，三个选项分别对应不同设施的今昔变化。
> - 选项A：The library has become larger 与原文一致；
> - 选项B：There were exercise machines at the old gym 与原文不符；
> - 选项C：The dining hall served rice noodles last year 与原文不符。
> 
> 【易错点】材料中会同时提到图书馆、体育馆、食堂三处设施，容易把"别处的变化"错记到某一处，要边听边对应地点。

8. Why does the speaker give the talk?

A. To get ideas on how to improve the school.
B. To share why the school needs to change.
C. To tell students about the school improvements.

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】C
> 
> 【考点】听力主旨与说话人意图
> 
> 【解析】
> Why does the speaker give the talk? 属于意图推断题，要听开头目的句和结尾总结句。
> - 选项A：To get ideas on how to improve the school 是征求建议，与原文不符；
> - 选项B：To share why the school needs to change 是说明原因，与原文不符；
> - 选项C：To tell students about the school improvements 与原文"向学生介绍学校改造情况"的意图一致。
> 
> 【易错点】A、B、C 三项都围绕"学校变化"，但说话人身份和对象不同，判断意图时要抓关键词 tell / inform / introduce。

10. Why does the speaker give the talk?

A. To explain the reasons for holding a show.

## 第二部分 知识运用（共20分）

### 四、单项选择（每小题1分，共12分）

17. My little brother Tom is very cute. We all enjoy playing with ___ .

A. him
B. her
C. you
D. them

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】A
> 
> 【考点】人称代词——宾格
> 
> 【解析】
> 介词 with 后需要人称代词的宾格形式，指代前文的 Tom（男孩）。
> - 选项A：him 是 he 的宾格，符合介词宾语的要求；
> - 选项B：her 是 she 的宾格，指女性，与 Tom 不符；
> - 选项C：you 指对方，与句意不符；
> - 选项D：them 是复数，不能指代单数的 Tom。
> 
> 【易错点】看到 Tom 就直接填 he，忽略介词后必须用宾格；him 与 her 要按性别区分。

18. Lily often visits her grandma ___ Sundays.

A. at
B. in
C. on
D. to

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】C
> 
> 【考点】时间介词——on 用于具体某天
> 
> 【解析】
> Sundays 是具体的星期几，时间介词用 on。
> - 选项A：at 用于具体时刻（at 7:00）；
> - 选项B：in 用于较长的时间段（in 2026, in October）；
> - 选项C：on 用于具体某一天或星期几，符合 Sundays；
> - 选项D：to 不是时间介词。
> 
> 【易错点】把 Sundays 当作"一周的时段"而误用 in；记忆口诀：at 时刻、on 某天、in 年月季节。

19. — Hi, Mike! Would you like to fly kites with me? — I'd like to, ___ I have to finish my question survey first.

A. so
B. and
C. but
D. or

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】C
> 
> 【考点】并列连词——转折关系
> 
> 【解析】
> I'd like to 表示"我愿意"，后半句 I have to finish my question survey first 表示"但我得先完成问卷"，前后是转折关系。
> - 选项A：so 表示因果，句意不通；
> - 选项B：and 表示并列递进，与后半句语义冲突；
> - 选项C：but 表示转折，符合"想去但不能去"的语气；
> - 选项D：or 表示选择或否则，句意不通。
> 
> 【易错点】看到 I'd like to 就以为一定选 and；要读完整句，抓住后半句的拒绝理由。

20. — ___ do some zoos have special events for kids? — To make it fun for them to learn about animals.

A. How
B. When
C. Where
D. Why

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】D
> 
> 【考点】特殊疑问词——Why 表原因
> 
> 【解析】
> 答语 To make it fun for them to learn about animals 表示目的/原因，用 Why 提问。
> - 选项A：How 问方式，答语应为 By doing...；
> - 选项B：When 问时间，答语应为时间状语；
> - 选项C：Where 问地点，答语应为地点状语；
> - 选项D：Why 问原因，答语用 To do... 或 Because...，符合。
> 
> 【易错点】把答语中的 to do 当作"方式"，误选 How；注意 Why 的答语常用不定式表目的。

21. — What are you going to give your mother for her birthday? — I am not sure. But I will buy her ___ .

A. something special
B. anything special
C. special something
D. special anything

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】A
> 
> 【考点】不定代词与形容词的位置
> 
> 【解析】
> 肯定句中用 something；形容词修饰 something、anything 等复合不定代词时必须后置。
> - 选项A：something special 语序正确，符合肯定句；
> - 选项B：anything special 中的 anything 多用于否定句和疑问句，本句是肯定句；
> - 选项C：special something 形容词位置错误；
> - 选项D：special anything 既有形容词位置错误，也不符合肯定句用法。
> 
> 【易错点】受汉语"特别的东西"影响，把形容词放到前面；记住"复合不定代词 + 形容词"的固定语序。

22. — Does Tara work as ___ as Tina? — Yes, she does.

A. hardly
B. hard
C. more hardly
D. harder

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】B
> 
> 【考点】副词的原级比较——as ... as
> 
> 【解析】
> as ... as 之间要用形容词或副词的原级，此处修饰动词 work，用副词 hard。
> - 选项A：hardly 意为"几乎不"，是另一个副词，句意不符；
> - 选项B：hard 作副词意为"努力地"，符合 as ... as 结构；
> - 选项C：more hardly 不存在的比较级形式；
> - 选项D：harder 是比较级，不能放在 as ... as 中间。
> 
> 【易错点】把 hard 当成形容词而误用 hardly（"几乎不"）；as ... as 中间永远用原级。

23. She has few friends. She is ___ than her sister.

A. serious
B. more serious
C. less serious
D. the most serious

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】B
> 
> 【考点】形容词比较级——than 结构
> 
> 【解析】
> 句中有 than，说明要用比较级；前句 She has few friends 说明她性格较严肃、不易接近。
> - 选项A：serious 是原级，不能与 than 连用；
> - 选项B：more serious 是比较级，符合 than 结构且与前句因果一致；
> - 选项C：less serious 语法正确，但与"朋友少"的原因不符；

Why "Useless" Science is So Important

Imagine a scientist who wins the Nobel Prize. You might picture them in a lab, solving a huge problem like fighting cancer or stopping climate change. But the story behind the 2025 Nobel Prizes, and so many before them, shows a more interesting truth. Often, the most world-changing discoveries begin not with a clear goal, but with a scientist asking a curious question that seems, at the time, totally useless.

This kind of exploration is called basic （基础的） research. Unlike designing a new phone or a faster car, basic research isn't about creating a product. It is simply the brave and wonderful act of trying to understand the world's deepest secrets—how life works, what matter is made of, and why things are the way they are.

The Nobel Prizes celebrated those discoveries. Take the prize in Medicine 2025 for example. One researcher, Shimon Sakaguchi, became curious about a few immune cells （免疫细胞） that didn't seem to fight germs （细菌）。At the time, studying these cells seemed like a waste of time. Yet, this curiosity-driven work finally $\underline{\text{revealed}}$ that these cells, later named regulatory T cells, are actually "peacekeepers" that stop the body from attacking itself after nearly thirty years. That "useless" knowledge later became the key to understanding and treating serious diseases where the body's immune system （系统） stops working correctly. Today, what started as a simple question is leading to new medicines that help retrain our body's immune system. It proves that asking a simple "why" about something small can end up protecting millions of people.

In another celebrated case, the Chemistry prize 2024 recognized work on designing and predicting （预测）the building blocks of life itself, which also began with questions that seemed impossible forever. For about 50 years, scientists wondered, “Can we design proteins （蛋白质）inside living things? Can a computer guess their 3-D shapes?” Back then, it was just knowledge and tools. They would never know from the very beginning that this long, basic research has provided a chance that allowed recent scientists to use AI to quickly map millions of proteins and create new ones, leading to new medicines and tools to deal with pollution.

The powerful lesson from the Nobel stage is clear: you cannot schedule a breakthrough. The next time you find yourself lost in a question that seems to have no point—why the stars shine, how a spider builds its perfect web, or what sound a tree makes—remember that you are thinking like a Nobel winner, for it is in the search of seemingly useless questions that we finally find the tools to build a better future.

提示词语：hobby, personality, understand, respect, learn

提示问题：
- Are you similar to or different from your friends?
- What do you have in common? /How are you different from them?
- What can similarities or differences bring to your friendship?

Hi everyone I'm happy to join in the discussion about "Similarities build connections, differences foster growth." ___

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】任选其一，范文见【解析】
> 
> 【考点】书面表达——命题作文（家庭成员责任 / 朋友间的异同）
> 
> 【解析】
> **题目 $①$ 写作要点**：全文用一般现在时；第一段承接所给开头句，点明"我为家庭做的事"；主体段列举 2–3 项具体家务（sweep the floor, clean the room, wash dishes），并用 because 说明理由（分担父母的辛苦、尽到 duty）；结尾回扣"家最温暖"的主题。
> 
> 范文（约 75 词）：
> Home is the warmest when every member adds their light. As a member of my family, I often do chores at home. For example, I sweep the floor and clean my room every weekend, and I help my parents wash the dishes after dinner. I want to do these things because I hope to share my parents' work and make them relax after a busy day. Looking after the family is not only a chore but also my duty. I feel proud when our home is clean and full of laughter.
> 
> **题目 $②$ 写作要点**：论坛发帖，语气可稍口语化；先表明"与朋友是相似还是不同"，再用 while / however 对比 personality 或 hobby；最后说明相似与差异对友谊的影响（understand, respect, learn from each other）。注意开头句已给出，不计入词数。
> 
> 范文（约 80 词）：
> Hi everyone I'm happy to join in the discussion about "Similarities build connections, differences foster growth." My best friend Li Lei and I are quite different. He is outgoing and loves sports, while I am quiet and enjoy reading. However, we share the same hobby—playing basketball—and we both care about others. I think differences make us learn from each other, and similarities help us understand each other better. Both of them make our friendship stronger and last longer.
> 
> 【易错点】① 未使用所给开头句、词数不足或只罗列家务而无理由；② 只写"相同"或只写"不同"，未回应提示问题第三问；两份范文都要避免出现真实校名和姓名。
