16. — Is this Emma's tennis racket? — No, it's not ___ . Ask Han Lin. I think it's his.

A. yours
<font color="#de7802">B. hers</font>
C. his
D. mine

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】B
> 
> 【考点】名词性物主代词的用法
> 
> 【解析】
> - 选项A：yours 意为"你的（东西）"，指代对方的物品，不符合语境；
> - 选项B：hers 意为"她的（东西）"，指代 Emma 的球拍，符合语境；
> - 选项C：his 意为"他的（东西）"，但后文说"I think it's his"，说明现在还不知道是谁的，排除；
> - 选项D：mine 意为"我的（东西）"，不符合语境。
> 
> 【易错点】名词性物主代词相当于"形容词性物主代词+名词"，单独使用，需根据上下文判断指代对象。

<font color="#de7802">A. at</font>
B. in
C. by
D. for

> 【答案】A
> 
> 【考点】时间介词的用法
> 
> 【解析】
> - 选项A：at weekends 意为"在周末"，英式用法，符合语境；
> - 选项B：in 通常用于 in the morning/afternoon/evening 或 in+月份/年份，不与 weekends 搭配；
> - 选项C：by 意为"到……为止"，不符合语境；

18. — I feel bored because I can't hang out with my friends this weekend. — I can't hang out ___ .

A. too
B. also
<font color="#de7802">C. either</font>
D. or

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】C
> 
> 【考点】否定句中"也"的表达方式
> 
> 【解析】
> - 选项A：too 用于肯定句句末，表示"也"，不符合否定句语境；
> - 选项B：also 用于肯定句中，通常放在实义动词之前、be动词之后，不符合否定句语境；
> - 选项C：either 用于否定句句末，表示"也"，符合语境；
> - 选项D：or 意为"或者"，用于否定句或疑问句中表示选择，不符合语境。
> 
> 【易错点】too/also/either 都表示"也"，但 too 用于肯定句句末，also 用于肯定句中，either 用于否定句句末。

### 题目 $①$

你校英文报刊正在举行 “文明校园，规则同行” 征文活动。假如你是李华，请用英文写一篇短文投稿，介绍三条及以上的校园规则，并阐述遵守这些规则的重要性。

提示词语：on time, respect, throw, safe, work together

提示问题：
- What rules do you have in your school?
- Why is it important to follow these rules?

To build a civilized campus, everyone needs to follow rules.

#### Example:
> To build a civilized campus, everyone needs to follow rules.
> 
> There are many important rules in our school. First, we must get to school and have classes **on time** and never be late. Second, we should **respect** our teachers and be friendly to classmates all the time. Third, we can’t **throw** rubbish everywhere and we need to keep the school clean. Besides, we have to keep quiet in the library and walk slowly in the hallways.
> 
> Following rules is really important. It can make our school **safe** and tidy. Also, it helps us learn to **work together** and develop good habits. Let’s follow school rules and make our campus more beautiful.


### 题目 $②$ 

你们学校将举办“Small Steps, Big changes（小行动，大改变）”的英文征文比赛。假如你是李华，请用英语写一篇短文，从饮食、体育锻炼等方面分享你为完善自我（improve myself）都做了哪些事，以及这样做的原因。

提示词语：cating habits, sports, work hard, healthy

提示问题：
1. What do you do to improve yourself?
2. Why do you do so?

Small daily actions can make a big difference to our life.

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

Li Hua

#### Example:
> Small daily actions can make a big difference to our life.
> To improve myself, I have made great changes in my daily life. First, I have developed good eating habits. I eat more fresh vegetables and fruit, and I stop eating junk food. Second, I take sports exercise every day, like running and playing ball games after school.
> 
> I keep doing these things because they can keep me healthy and energetic. A strong body helps me study better and work hard at my lessons. I believe small steps will bring me big changes in the future.
> 
> Li Hua

> 【解析】
> 题目①写作要点：
> - 审题：介绍三条及以上校园规则 + 阐述遵守规则的重要性，属于说明文。
> - 结构：开头点题 → 分条列举规则（First/Second/Third/Besides） → 总结重要性。
> - 时态：一般现在时为主，使用 must/should/can't 等情态动词。
> - 注意：必须使用提示词语 on time, respect, throw, safe, work together。
> 
> 题目②写作要点：
> - 审题：从饮食、体育锻炼等方面分享为完善自我做了什么 + 原因，属于记叙文。
> - 结构：开头引入 → 具体做法（First/Second） → 原因阐述 → 结尾展望。
> - 时态：一般现在时和现在完成时为主。
> - 注意：必须使用提示词语 eating habits, sports, work hard, healthy。
> 
> 【易错点】
> - 注意词数不少于70词，所给内容不计入总词数；
> - 不能写出真实校名和姓名；
> - 注意使用连接词（First/Second/Besides/Also）使文章条理清晰；
> - 题目①注意规则条数至少三条；题目②注意从饮食和体育锻炼两方面展开。
