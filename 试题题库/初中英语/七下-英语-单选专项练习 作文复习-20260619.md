# 单项选择

## 一、单项选择

1. Everyone ___ excited when they saw the amazing waterfall.

A. is
B. are
C. was
D. were

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> **【答案】** C
> **【考点】** 一般过去时 + 不定代词作主语的主谓一致
> **【解析】**
> 句意：当他们看到令人惊叹的瀑布时，每个人都兴奋不已。
> 原句：Everyone ___ excited when they saw the amazing waterfall.
> 
> 选项分析：
> - A. is 一般现在时单数，时态不符
> - B. are 一般现在时复数，时态和数都不符
> - C. was 一般过去时单数，正确
> - D. were 一般过去时复数，everyone是单数，不符
> 
> 综上，C选项符合题意，故正确答案为C。
> **【易错点】** 误选D：认为 everyone 指代多个人所以用复数。实际上 everyone 作主语时谓语用单数。

> - D. is lying 现在进行时，正确
> 
> 综上，D选项符合题意，故正确答案为D。
> **【易错点】** 误选A：lying 是现在分词，但不能单独作谓语，必须有 be 动词构成进行时态。

## 单选专项练习（一）

5. 旅行教会了我最甜蜜的回报往往紧随奋斗之后。

This journey told us that sweetest reward follow struggle work.

It teaches me to face difficulties bravely. I really like this school trip.

### 讲中国故事（Unit 8）

#### 参考作文6-7 题型示例

假如你是李华，你校近期有一个传统故事会，你邀请老外来参加，请用英语写一封邮件，告诉参加的时间地点，活动的内容以及对他的一些建议

提示问题
- When and where is the story-telling?
- What is about the story-telling?
- How will she prepare for it?

#### 基础版

Dear Friend,

I'm writing to invite you to our school's Traditional Storytelling Event! It will be held in the school hall at 3 p.m. next Friday, July 5th.

This event is to spread Chinese traditional culture. Students will share classic stories like “Journey to the West” and so on. After each story, there will be a discussion to exchange ideas.

To prepare, you can choose a Chinese traditional story you're interested in and tell it for us. Don't worry if your Chinese isn't perfect—you can tell the story in English!

I believe you'll have a great time. Looking forward to your reply!

Best wishes.

Li Hun

#### 挑战版

Dear Friend,

I'm excited to invite you to our upcoming Traditional Storytelling Event! It will take place in the school hall at 2:00 p.m. on June 30th, Saturday. This event is a great chance to explore Chinese culture through fascinating stories.

During the event, students will share classic tales, including legends about historical heroes, magical folk stories, and moral fables. For example, you might hear about “The Monkey King’s adventures” from Journcy to the West or “Mulan’s brave act of taking her father’s place in the army.” After each story, we’ll have lively discussions where everyone can share their thoughts and feelings.

To prepare, I suggest choosing a story that interests you first. And you can speak slowly so we can understand you. Adding some facial expressions or body language can make your storytelling more attractive. Don't worry if your Chinese isn't perfect—you can tell the story in English.

I’m sure you’ll enjoy this cultural journey. Looking forward to seeing you there!

Best wishes.

3. 这次活动是一个很好的机会通过讲故事来探索中国文化。

This event is a great chance to explore Chinese culture through fascinating stories.

This event is to spread Chinese traditional culture.

I’m sure you’ll enjoy this cultural journey.
