若 $a^{12} = (a^{3})^{m} = a^{2} \cdot a^{n}$，则 $m=$ ___ ；$n=$ ___ 。

> [!question]- 点击查看答案与解析
> 【答案】$m=4$，$n=10$
> 
> 【解析】
> - $(a^{3})^{m} = a^{3m} = a^{12}$，$\therefore 3m = 12$，$m = 4$
> - $a^{2} \cdot a^{n} = a^{2+n} = a^{12}$，$\therefore 2+n = 12$，$n = 10$
>
